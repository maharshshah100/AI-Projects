=== ANNUAL NET REVENUE HISTORY -- ACCOUNTING NOTE ===

JPMorgan Chase reported net revenue of $158.1 billion in 2023, a then-record high for the company. IMPORTANT ACCOUNTING NOTE: bank "revenue" is fundamentally not comparable to a technology or consumer company's revenue. Banks report net revenue (interest income minus interest expense, plus fee-based income), not gross sales, because their core business is lending money and managing risk, not selling a product at a fixed price. Comparing a bank's net revenue directly against a tech company's gross revenue significantly understates the bank's actual scale of financial activity.
