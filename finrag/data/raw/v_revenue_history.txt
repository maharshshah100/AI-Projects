=== ANNUAL REVENUE HISTORY ===

Visa annual revenue by fiscal year (fiscal year ends September; dollars in billions): FY2023 $32.65, FY2024 $35.93 (+10.0%). Visa's revenue is driven primarily by payments volume, transaction processing fees, and international transaction/cross-border fees -- Visa itself does not issue cards or extend credit; it operates the payments network that banks and merchants use.
