=== ANNUAL REVENUE HISTORY ===

Netflix annual revenue by calendar year (dollars in billions): 2020 $25.0, 2021 $29.7 (+18.8%), 2022 $31.6 (+6.5%, growth slowed sharply amid subscriber saturation in mature markets), 2023 $33.72 (+6.7%), 2024 $39.0 (+15.6%, reaccelerated on ad-tier subscriber growth and the password-sharing crackdown converting freeloaders to paid accounts).

Netflix's growth trajectory shows a clear pattern: rapid pandemic-era growth (2020-2021), a sharp slowdown (2022) as the streaming market matured and competition intensified, followed by a deliberate strategic reacceleration (2023-2024) driven by two specific initiatives -- the ad-supported tier and password-sharing enforcement.
