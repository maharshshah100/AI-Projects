=== ANNUAL REVENUE HISTORY ===

Tesla annual revenue by calendar year (dollars in billions): 2023 $96.77, 2024 $97.69 (+0.9%, roughly flat). Growth slowed considerably compared to Tesla's historical pace amid intensified EV competition, multiple rounds of price cuts to defend market share, and softer demand growth in key markets.

Tesla's revenue includes automotive sales/leasing, energy generation and storage, and services. Automotive remains the large majority of total revenue.
