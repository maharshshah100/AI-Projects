=== ANNUAL REVENUE HISTORY ===

Meta Platforms annual revenue by calendar year (dollars in billions): 2023 $134.90, 2024 $164.50 (+21.9%). Growth in 2024 was driven by digital advertising demand and improved ad targeting and pricing, following a slower growth period in 2022 amid a broader digital ad market slowdown and rising competition for user attention.

Meta's revenue is overwhelmingly derived from advertising across Facebook, Instagram, and its other apps. Reality Labs (VR/AR/metaverse investments) represents a small fraction of revenue but a large share of operating losses, reflecting Meta's long-term bet on the category.
