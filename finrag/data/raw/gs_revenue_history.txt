=== ANNUAL NET REVENUE HISTORY -- ACCOUNTING NOTE ===

Goldman Sachs annual net revenue (dollars in billions, approximate): 2021 (peak) $59.34, declining in subsequent years amid a slower investment banking and trading environment, then recovering to approximately $58.28 by 2025. Like JPMorgan Chase, this is net revenue, not gross sales -- Goldman Sachs is an investment bank, and its revenue is driven by trading, investment banking fees, and asset/wealth management, not product sales. Direct comparison to a non-bank's revenue is not meaningful without accounting for this difference.
