=== ANNUAL REVENUE HISTORY ===

Nvidia annual revenue by fiscal year (fiscal year ends late January; dollars in billions): FY2023 (ended Jan 2023) $26.97, FY2024 (ended Jan 2024) $60.92 (+126%, more than doubling year-over-year). This growth was driven overwhelmingly by AI data center GPU demand, particularly for training and running large language models, representing one of the fastest large-scale revenue ramps of any major public company in recent history.

Nvidia's revenue is concentrated in its Data Center segment (AI/ML infrastructure GPUs), with Gaming, Professional Visualization, and Automotive as smaller segments.
