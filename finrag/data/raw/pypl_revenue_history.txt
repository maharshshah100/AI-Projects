=== ANNUAL REVENUE HISTORY ===

PayPal annual net revenue (dollars in billions): FY2021 $25.4 (+18%), FY2024 $31.79. PayPal's revenue growth decelerated substantially after 2021 as e-commerce growth normalized following the pandemic-era surge, and competition in digital payments intensified from both traditional card networks and newer fintech entrants.
